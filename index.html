SynopsisOne night. One warning.

Aiza has a habit of ignoring her phone after midnight. Tonight it will not stop ringing. At 3:17 a.m. she answers, and the voice on the line is her own, tired and certain, calling from tomorrow.

The caller knows a mistake Aiza will make at sunrise. Each warning she follows changes what happens next, and each change costs her something she didn’t expect to lose.
